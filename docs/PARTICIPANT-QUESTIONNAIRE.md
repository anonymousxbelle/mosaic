# Mosaic User Study Feedback

[Open the questionnaire](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdYNjkKFDr58bBdMEukDVlM6zW_gObwy1dTFJdTPkkwj2n0VA/viewform)

Mosaic combines your ratings of books, movies and TV shows into a taste profile, then uses it to recommend other titles. 

This study asks whether those recommendations fit your interests and/or introduce you to media you would not normally explore. 

For this user study, we ask that you:
a. Add and rate at least 5 titles you already know, including at least 2 media types (for example, books and TV). 
b. Use Discover → For You with All media selected.
c. Use Discover → Based On with media and filters of your choice.
d. Look at the first 5 recommendations, read their descriptions and explanations, and save any you would like to try. If fewer than 5 results appear, evaluate the results available. 
e. Then complete this form about that experience. 

Participation is voluntary; feedback questions may be skipped. Responses will be used for the class project and presentation.

After completing the For You activity above, you are encouraged to explore the rest of Mosaic, including Based On, My Library, Collections, and preferences. Any general feedback about your overall experience is highly appreciated.
