# Questionnaire status — November 11, 2026

The Google Form now exists and has been revised around mixed-media For You profiles:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1OpdAc-28Jzrhg_WEau10KmTUZekG9Z9GQ-C5WvgflR4/edit

Current instructions: rate at least five known titles spanning two media, use For You / All, inspect the first five recommendations, and complete once about that experience. The old ambiguous “assigned task” and “starting title and task” wording is removed. The requested trailing “Please avoid...” text was removed.

Measures: names/email; actual library inputs; overall relevance, discovery value, explanation clarity, intent and ease (1–5); chosen title; usual media habits; prior awareness/consideration; willingness to explore a less-usual medium; actual enjoyment only if tried; problems; follow-up and quote preferences. The form remains unpublished and a real test submission is still pending. It is an experience survey, not yet a controlled single-medium vs mixed-media comparison instrument.
