# User-study questionnaire

Updated November 13, 2026. The owner has reported participant use of the form. Earlier November 11 notes saying it was unpublished are historical, not its current reported status. The owner has edited the form; the outline below summarizes the study topics and is not a verbatim export of the latest wording.

[Google Form — owner editing link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1OpdAc-28Jzrhg_WEau10KmTUZekG9Z9GQ-C5WvgflR4/edit). Use the form's responder link when inviting participants; this repository does not record responses or contact details.

## Participant activity

Rate at least five familiar titles spanning at least two supported media types, select For You / All media, inspect the first five recommendations, then complete the questionnaire once about that experience. Additional exploration of Based On, Library and Collections is encouraged.

## Questions and measures covered

| Topic | What participants report |
| --- | --- |
| Participant information | Name, email and follow-up preferences. |
| Starting preferences | Titles rated and the media they normally consume. |
| Relevance | How well the recommendations match their interests (1–5). |
| Discovery value | Whether recommendations introduced something new or previously unconsidered (1–5 and title-specific questions). |
| Explanation clarity | Whether match explanations helped them understand the suggestions (1–5). |
| Intent to try | Interest in reading/watching a recommended title (1–5). |
| Cross-media discovery | Interest in a media type they would not usually explore. |
| Usability | Ease of use, navigation problems and general feedback beyond the primary activity. |
| Follow-up | Whether they actually tried a recommendation and enjoyed it, if applicable. |
| Reporting permission | Follow-up and quotation preferences. |

This questionnaire measures perceived usefulness and discovery. It does not by itself prove mixed-media profiles outperform single-media profiles. See the [study plan](USER-STUDY.md) for the proposed comparison and reporting approach, and the [feedback log](FEEDBACK-CHANGES.md) for reported observations. Do not substitute intent to try for actual enjoyment.
