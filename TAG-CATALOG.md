## Other signals and limitations

- Media categories: Book, TV, Movie; game support remains in code and music is paused. Music and game vocabulary above is retained.
- Provider genres/keywords, synopsis, creator, publication year, provider rank and optional AI similarity contribute data or ranking signals.
- Series identity and position help distinguish sequels and diversify results.
- User ratings, avoided genres, personal tags and feedback are separate from the fixed vocabulary. User-created tags are open-ended and cannot be exhaustively listed.
- Audience labels currently cover middle-grade and young-adult only. Content certifications and adult-content flags are separate from intended audience; age verification is not implemented.
- Settings and premises are a limited rule-based vocabulary, not comprehensive understanding of every story.
